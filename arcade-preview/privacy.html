Privacy Policy

StratoshiVibes Arcade · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Summary

StratoshiVibes Arcade is a free collection of games (Mahjong, Battlegrounds, Rugpull Poker, Escape the Rug). It does not ask for an account and does not collect or sell personal data. Connecting a wallet is optional.

What is stored, and where

To let you resume a game and keep your best scores, the game saves a few small values on your own device using the browser's local storage:

  • games in progress (for example Mahjong tile positions or an Escape the Rug run)
  • your progress and best results in each game
  • your Degen Passport: level, badges, daily challenge results and the name you choose for it
  • your settings: language, sound on or off, game options
  • if you connect a wallet: your public wallet address, so your holder tiles show next time

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, login, or email collection
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
  • No transactions or signing requests, except a payment you start yourself in the Arcade Shop
  • No ads, no tokens, no real-money rewards. Optional purchases in the Arcade Shop only

Optional wallet connection

You can connect a Solana wallet (for example Seed Vault Wallet on Seeker, Phantom, or Solflare) to show the StratoshiVibes Vol.1 Degens you own in gold in every game (for example as gold holo tiles in Mahjong). The game receives only your public address.It only asks you to approve a transaction when you buy an item in the Arcade Shop, and it never asks you to sign a message.

To find your Degens, the game sends your public address to a Solana data provider (Helius) and reads the public list of assets in that wallet, including your SKR balance for the Passport's SKR badge. Public addresses and their assets are already visible to anyone on the Solana blockchain. You can disconnect at any time, which also removes the saved address from your device.

Arcade Shop

If you buy an item, the game builds one transfer to the Arcade's wallet with a short note naming the item, and your wallet asks you to approve it. To show prices, the game asks a public price service (Jupiter) for the current SOL and SKR prices; no personal data is sent. To restore purchases, the game reads the public transaction history of your connected address through Helius. The list of what you bought is also saved on your device.

Hosting

The game files are served from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
